# Pointly vertical master

Purpose: retain the complete merchant-to-customer loyalty story in a portrait product launch film. Source of truth: the approved 63.8-second horizontal master. Native 1080×1920, 60 FPS, 3828 frames; all ten scene durations and interaction cue frames retained.

The existing Pointly logo, Google Sans, blue/pale-blue palette, rounded controls, products, rewards, tier rules, copy, demo ledger and original audio are reused locally. No new product functionality is introduced. Promotional headings and explanatory callouts sit outside white product surfaces.

Portrait direction: generous top/bottom margins, large two-line headings, focused merchant sections, vertically stacked cards, independently framed modals, and customer screens occupying most of the canvas. Desktop navigation is a controlled crop of the real tab row. Do not scale a whole desktop frame into portrait.

Scene graph (start frame / frames):

2. 192 / 330 — branch detail becomes rules, products and reward stack.
3. 522 / 390 — large QR resolves into a compact QR beside a downward connector; registration phone rises underneath.
4. 912 / 396 — portrait purchase modal, then sale result and large +100 card.
5. 1308 / 468 — the same +100 card moves into the balance view, followed by a vertical catalog scroll.
6. 1776 / 408 — preserve the catalog position, switch to the merchant reward, confirm redemption and show zero balance.
7. 2184 / 420 — customer balance and tier progress above a stacked tier ladder.
8. 2604 / 372 — stacked promotion configuration becomes a tall 2x card, carried into the customer catalog.
9. 2976 / 612 — inactive customer, portrait template with resolving variables, then large WhatsApp draft.
10. 3588 / 240 — merchant/customer cards recede vertically around a centered Pointly CTA.

Motion: preserve spring/easing character and native frame timing; redesign cursor coordinates with each control. QR-to-phone, award-to-balance, catalog-to-redemption and merchant-to-customer campaign are continuity anchors. No generic full-screen fades. Reuse the original music/SFX because action frames are unchanged.

Verification: scene starts/ends and action frames, portrait safe area/readability, clipping/stacking, exact source ledger, matching cue schedules, compatible 4.0.530 packages, Studio preview and still QA. No MP4 rendering. Horizontal source is hash-checked and must remain unchanged.
